# Privacy and Ethics Disclaimer

## Important Notice

This software is designed for **research and educational purposes only**. It is not intended for production use in biometric identification or voice cloning applications.

## Privacy Safeguards

### Local Processing
- All audio processing is performed locally on your device
- No audio data is transmitted to external servers
- Uploaded files are processed in memory and not permanently stored

### Data Protection
- Optional filename anonymization to remove identifying information
- Automatic removal of personally identifiable information from logs
- No collection or storage of personal data

### Research Focus
- Designed for academic and research applications
- Suitable for studying speech segmentation algorithms
- Intended for educational demonstrations

## Ethical Guidelines

### Prohibited Uses
- **Do not use for biometric identification in production systems**
- **Do not use for voice cloning or deepfake generation**
- **Do not use for surveillance or monitoring without consent**
- **Do not use for commercial voice analysis without proper authorization**

### Responsible Use
- Respect privacy and consent when processing audio data
- Use only for legitimate research and educational purposes
- Ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations
- Consider the ethical implications of your research

## Technical Limitations

### Accuracy Limitations
- This is a research demonstration, not a production system
- Results may not be suitable for critical applications
- Performance may vary significantly across different audio types
- No guarantee of accuracy or reliability

### Security Considerations
- This software is not designed for security-critical applications
- No encryption or secure processing guarantees
- Users are responsible for securing their data and systems

## Compliance

### Legal Compliance
- Users must comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Consider data protection laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
- Respect intellectual property rights
- Obtain proper consent for audio processing

### Academic Use
- Suitable for academic research and education
- Can be used for algorithm development and testing
- Appropriate for conference presentations and publications
